title: Content Model
description: Sanity document types currently used by the Pizza Hut Studio.
---

The Studio schemas are located in `apps/studio/schemaTypes`.

## Document Types

- `homePage` — homepage content and configurable sections.
- `menu` — menu-level content and menu presentation data.
- `category` — product grouping and category metadata.
- `product` — product name, description, price, image, rating, and related menu data.
- `reservations` — reservation-related content and configuration.

The schema registry is assembled in `schemaTypes/index.ts`. When the schemas change, update generated types and verify the web queries that consume the affected documents.
